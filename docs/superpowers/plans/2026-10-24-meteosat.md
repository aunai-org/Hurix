# Meteosat over Europe, Africa and the Indian Ocean — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Close the gap between about 30° W and 80° E with live Meteosat-12 and Meteosat-9 imagery everywhere the pipeline uses satellites: the stitched map, the HD tiles, the 24 h globe, storm close-ups, loops and tracks, true or natural colour, and cloud-top height.

**Architecture:**
- A new `pipeline/meteosat.py` owns everything specific to EUMETView WMS: requests, cache, calibration tables and the colour decoders.
- It hands the rest of the pipeline a `GridDisc` (a regular lat/lon raster) with the same `sample(lat, lon)` interface as `storms.Disc`.
- `storms`, `reproject`, `globe` and `visible` route the two Meteosats through it. The page only gains credits and the natural-colour wording.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy, Pillow, netCDF4, urllib), TypeScript/Vite/vitest, pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-meteosat-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Service:** `https://view.eumetsat.int/geoserver/ows`. WMS 1.3.0, `crs=EPSG:4326`, bbox in **lat,lon** order (`lat0,lon0,lat1,lon1`), `format=image/png`, `TIME=YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:00.000Z`.
- **Pieces:** at most 2048 px a side per request. Up to 4 requests in flight. Timeout 60 s, 3 tries.
- **Meteosat-12:** `mtg_fd:ir105_hrfi`, sub-satellite 0.0°, every 10 min. Raw counts via an identity `SLD_BODY` (0→#000000, 255→#ffffff, ramp). Count 0 = no data.
- **Meteosat-9:** `msg_iodc:ir108`, sub-satellite 45.5° E, every 15 min. Default style only: grey, dark = warm. It rejects `SLD_BODY`.
- **Cloud-top height:** `msg_fes:cth` (used for Meteosat-12's area) and `msg_iodc:cth`, both every 15 min. Decoded to **metres**, like GOES ACHA `HT`.
- **Colour:**
  - `mtg_fd:rgb_truecolour` gives `visKind` "true". It goes to Meteosat-12 storms, and to Meteosat-9 storms where Meteosat-12's `view_cos` ≥ 0.3.
  - `msg_iodc:rgb_natural` gives `"visKind": "natural"`. It goes to the other Meteosat-9 storms.
- **Display names:** "Meteosat-12", "Meteosat-9". Credit "© EUMETSAT".
- **Copy, exactly:**
  - satellite line: `LIVE IR · Himawari-9 · GOES-18 · GOES-19 · Meteosat-12 · Meteosat-9 © EUMETSAT`
  - natural hint: `Natural colour from Meteosat-9: a false-colour mix, land green and ice clouds cyan`
- **Failure:** Meteosat never fails a run. Log `<sat>: skipped (<reason>)`; that area falls back to today's behaviour.
- **Calibration targets:** IR median abs residual under about 3 K; cloud-top height median abs difference against ACHA under about 1 km.
- **Repo rules:** no Claude session links or ids in commits or PRs. Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 (1M context) <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **EUMETView slow, down or throttling mid-run.** The run finishes on time without Meteosat, and every failing piece of work logs `skipped`. Pinned by `test_load_sats_survives_wms_failure` (Task 4) and `test_image_raises_not_found_on_service_exception` (Task 2).
2. **Latest slot not published yet** (EUMETView lags about 20–30 min). The close-up and the globe fall back to the previous slot. Pinned by `test_closeup_near_steps_back_one_meteosat_slot` (Task 3).
3. **Cache reuse across different boxes or sizes for the same time.** A cached image is only reused for the identical request. Pinned by `test_cache_key_depends_on_every_request_field` (Task 2).
4. **Calibration drifting** if EUMETSAT changes its rendering. Each run prints a per-satellite overlap bias against the neighbour and warns over 5 K. Pinned by `test_overlap_bias` (Task 4).
5. **Coloured overlay pixels and off-disc black** in Meteosat-9's grey image. These must not become 299 K cloud-free sea at the disc edge. Pinned by `test_grey_to_bt_masks_coloured_pixels` and `test_griddisc_masks_off_disc` (Task 1).

### Task 1: `meteosat.py` core (no network)

**Files:**
- Create: `pipeline/meteosat.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `SATS: dict[str, dict]`, keys `meteosat12` and `meteosat9`. Fields: `name`, `lon0`, `step` (min), `ir` (layer), `raw` (bool), `cth` (layer), `true` (`"mtg_fd:rgb_truecolour"`), `natural` (`"msg_iodc:rgb_natural"` or `None`).
  - `slot(sat: str, when: datetime) -> datetime`: the latest multiple of `step` minutes at or before `when`.
  - `wms_params(layer: str, when: datetime, box: tuple[float, float, float, float], w: int, h: int, raw: bool) -> dict[str, str]`, where `box = (lat0, lat1, lon0, lon1)`.
  - `pieces(box, w, h, max_px=2048) -> list[tuple[box, w, h, row, col]]`: tiles the request with the row/col offset of each piece; the pieces add up exactly to `w × h`.
  - `to_bt(sat: str, rgb: np.ndarray, cal: dict) -> np.ndarray` (float32 K, NaN for no data).
    - Raw sats: count = channel 0; 0 → NaN; else `cal["ir"][sat][count]`.
    - Grey sats: NaN where R, G and B differ; else `cal["ir"][sat][grey]`.
  - `decode_cth(rgb: np.ndarray, cal: dict) -> np.ndarray` (float32 metres): look up `(r>>4, g>>4, b>>4)` in `cal["cth"]` (list of `[r4, g4, b4, metres_or_null]`). Unknown colours and `null` → NaN.
  - `class GridDisc(img, lat0, lat1, lon0, lon1, lon_sub)`:
    - `img[row, col]` with row 0 at `lat1` (north), pixel centres spread evenly over the box;
    - `.sample(lat, lon) -> float32` is bilinear and accepts arrays that broadcast;
    - NaN outside the box, where `img` is NaN, or where `storms.view_cos(lat, lon, lon_sub) < 0.05`;
    - `lon` is wrapped into `[lon0, lon0 + 360)`.
  - `class GridRGB(img_uint8_hw3, lat0, lat1, lon0, lon1, lon_sub)`: same geometry. `.sample(lat, lon) -> uint8 (..., 3)` is bilinear per channel, and gives 0 outside the box or off the disc.
  - `box_for(lat: float, lon: float, km: float) -> tuple`: the lat/lon bounds of `storms.grid_latlon(lat, lon, 32, km * 1.1)`.

    - fetches the pieces 4 at a time;

  - `world(sat, when, deg: float) -> GridDisc`: BT over `(-75, 75, lon0 - 78, lon0 + 78)` at `deg` degrees a pixel.
  - `cth_world(sat, when, deg: float) -> GridDisc`: metres over the same box, from `SATS[sat]["cth"]` at `slot(…, step 15)`.
  - `rgb_disc(layer, sat, when, lat, lon, km=2048.0, px_km=1.0) -> GridRGB`.
  - CLI `python3 meteosat.py calibrate DATE` (DATE = YYYY-MM-DD).
